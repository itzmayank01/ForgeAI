# Port Call Procedure — Operations Manual Section 3

3.1 The agent must submit the pre-arrival notification no later than **72 hours**
before the vessel's estimated time of arrival.

3.2 A revised notification is required whenever the ETA moves by more than
**6 hours**.

3.3 The Master's declaration must be lodged within **2 hours** of berthing.

3.4 Crew lists are submitted in duplicate; one copy is retained by the agency for
**7 years**.

3.5 Where a vessel arrives outside office hours, an out-of-hours attendance fee
of **EUR 340** applies.

3.6 Shifting a vessel between berths requires **4 hours** notice to the harbour
master.

3.7 The agency retains port call files for **5 years** from departure.
