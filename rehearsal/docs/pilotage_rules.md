# Pilotage Requirements — Operations Manual Section 6

6.1 Pilotage is compulsory for all vessels exceeding **90 metres** length overall.

6.2 Masters holding a valid Pilotage Exemption Certificate may navigate without a
pilot provided the vessel does not exceed **120 metres**.

6.3 A pilot must be booked a minimum of **12 hours** in advance.

6.4 Cancellation within **4 hours** of the booked time incurs **50%** of the
pilotage fee.

6.5 Pilot boarding takes place at the outer station in weather up to Beaufort
force **6**; above that the inner station is used.

6.6 A second pilot is required for vessels exceeding **250 metres** or carrying
dangerous goods in bulk.
