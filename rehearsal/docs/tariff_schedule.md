# Tariff Schedule 2026 — Rotterdam and Antwerp only

4.1 Port dues are charged at **EUR 0.42 per gross tonne** for the first call in a
calendar month.

4.2 Subsequent calls within the same calendar month attract **EUR 0.31 per gross
tonne**.

4.3 Pilotage is **EUR 1,850** for vessels up to 180 metres length overall.

4.4 Vessels exceeding 180 metres attract pilotage of **EUR 2,640**.

4.5 Towage is quoted per job and is **not** included in the tariff.

4.6 Mooring gangs are charged at **EUR 580** per operation, doubled between
22:00 and 06:00.

4.7 Waste reception is **EUR 95** per cubic metre, minimum **3** cubic metres.

4.8 All tariffs exclude VAT and are payable within **21 days** of invoice.
