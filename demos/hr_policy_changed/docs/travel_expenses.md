# Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Policy reference:** FIN-11 · **Effective:** 1 January 2026

## 1. Per-diem allowances

1.1 Domestic travel attracts a per-diem of **EUR 72** per full day.

1.2 International travel attracts a per-diem of **EUR 110** per full day.

1.3 Travel to designated high-cost ports (Singapore, Oslo, Tokyo, Zurich)
attracts a per-diem of **EUR 130** per full day.

1.4 A part day of **6 hours or more** is claimed at 50% of the applicable rate.
A part day under 6 hours is not claimable.

## 2. Receipts

2.1 Receipts are required for any single item of **EUR 40 or more**.

2.2 Items under EUR 40 may be claimed without a receipt up to a monthly
aggregate of **EUR 200**.

2.3 Claims submitted without required receipts are rejected and returned.

## 3. Accommodation

3.1 Accommodation is reimbursed at cost up to **EUR 180** per night domestic and
**EUR 260** per night international.

3.2 Bookings above these caps require advance written approval from the budget
holder.

## 4. Transport

4.1 Economy class is the standard for all flights under **6 hours**.

4.2 Premium economy may be booked for flights of 6 hours or more with budget
holder approval.

4.3 Private car use is reimbursed at **EUR 0.38 per kilometre**.

4.4 Taxi fares are reimbursed where public transport is unavailable or where the
journey falls between 22:00 and 06:00.

## 5. Approval limits

5.1 Line managers may approve claims up to **EUR 1,500**.

5.2 Heads of Department may approve claims up to **EUR 7,500**.

5.3 Claims above EUR 7,500 require Finance Director approval.

## 6. Submission deadline

6.1 Claims must be submitted within **30 days** of the final day of travel.

6.2 Claims submitted between 31 and 60 days require written justification.

6.3 Claims submitted after **60 days** are not reimbursed.
