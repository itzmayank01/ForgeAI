# Grievance Procedure

**Policy reference:** HR-09 · **Effective:** 1 January 2026

## 1. Scope

1.1 This procedure covers complaints about working conditions, treatment by
colleagues or managers, application of company policy, and health and safety
concerns.

1.2 Complaints of bullying, harassment or discrimination follow this procedure
but are escalated directly to Stage 2.

## 2. Stage 1 — Informal resolution

2.1 Staff are encouraged to raise the matter directly with their line manager
within **15 working days** of the incident.

2.2 The line manager must acknowledge within **3 working days** and hold an
informal discussion within **10 working days**.

2.3 Where the grievance concerns the line manager, it may be raised with the
next manager in the reporting line.

## 3. Stage 2 — Formal grievance

3.1 A formal grievance must be submitted in writing to HR using form HR-09a.

3.2 HR acknowledges receipt within **5 working days**.

3.3 A grievance hearing is convened within **20 working days** of receipt.

3.4 The member of staff may be accompanied by a colleague or a trade union
representative.

3.5 A written outcome is issued within **10 working days** of the hearing.

## 4. Stage 3 — Appeal

4.1 An appeal must be lodged within **10 working days** of the written outcome.

4.2 The appeal is heard by a manager not previously involved, within **20
working days**.

4.3 The appeal outcome is final within the company procedure.

## 5. Records

5.1 Grievance records are retained for **6 years** from the date of the final
outcome.

5.2 Records are confidential and disclosed only to those directly involved in
the procedure.

## 6. Protection

6.1 No member of staff will be disadvantaged for raising a grievance in good
faith.

6.2 Grievances found to have been raised maliciously may be treated as a
disciplinary matter.
