# Annual and Parental Leave Policy — Shore Staff

**Policy reference:** HR-04 · **Effective:** 1 January 2026 · **Review:** annually

## 1. Annual leave entitlement

1.1 Shore-based staff accrue **22 days** of paid annual leave per calendar year,
accruing at 1.83 days per completed month of service.

1.2 Staff with more than **five years** of continuous service accrue **26 days**
per calendar year.

1.3 Part-time staff accrue leave pro rata, rounded up to the nearest half day.

1.4 Public holidays are additional and are not deducted from annual leave.

## 2. Carry-over

2.1 A maximum of **5 days** of unused annual leave may be carried into the
following calendar year.

2.2 Carried-over leave must be taken by **31 March** or it is forfeited.

2.3 Carry-over above 5 days requires written approval from a Head of Department
and is granted only where operational demands prevented leave being taken.

## 3. Requesting leave

3.1 Requests must be submitted at least **10 working days** before the intended
start date. Requests of 10 consecutive days or more require **20 working days**
notice.

3.2 Line managers must respond within **5 working days** of receiving a request.

3.3 Where two staff in the same team request overlapping dates, the earlier
submitted request takes precedence.

## 4. Parental leave

4.1 The primary caregiver is entitled to **16 weeks** of fully paid parental
leave.

4.2 The secondary caregiver is entitled to **4 weeks** of fully paid parental
leave, which must be taken within **12 months** of the birth or placement.

4.3 Parental leave may be taken in up to **three** separate blocks with the
agreement of the line manager.

4.4 Annual leave continues to accrue during parental leave.

4.5 Staff must give **8 weeks** notice of their intended start date where
practicable.

## 5. Unpaid leave

5.1 Unpaid leave of up to **20 working days** per year may be granted at the
discretion of a Head of Department.

5.2 Unpaid leave does not count toward continuous service for the purposes of
clause 1.2.

## 6. Sickness during leave

6.1 Where a member of staff falls ill during booked annual leave and provides a
medical certificate, the affected days are reclassified as sick leave and
returned to the annual leave balance.
