# Supply Agreement — Kowloon Diesel Parts Ltd

**Agreement reference:** SUP-KDP-2026 · **Effective:** 15 January 2026 ·
**Term:** 12 months, auto-renewing

## 1. Minimum order

1.1 The minimum order quantity is **5 units** per line item.

1.2 The minimum order value is **EUR 1,200** excluding freight.

1.3 Mixed-category orders count toward the value minimum but not the unit
minimum.

## 2. Volume discount tiers

2.1 Discounts apply per order, not cumulatively:

| Order value | Discount |
|---|---|
| EUR 1,200 – 14,999 | **2%** |
| EUR 15,000 – 59,999 | **6%** |
| EUR 60,000 and above | **9%** |

2.2 An additional **1.5%** applies to orders placed more than 45 days ahead of
required delivery.

## 3. Payment terms

3.1 Payment is due **45 days** from bill of lading date.

3.2 New accounts operate on prepayment for the first **three** orders.

3.3 Overdue amounts accrue interest at **1.2% per month**.

## 4. Warranty

4.1 Diesel engine components carry an **18 month** warranty from date of
delivery or **3,000 running hours**, whichever occurs first.

4.2 Electrical components carry a **12 month** warranty.

4.3 Warranty claims require the original part to be returned for inspection
within **45 days** of the claim being raised.

## 5. Returns

5.1 Returns are accepted within **21 days** of delivery.

5.2 Restocking fee is **20%**, reduced to **10%** where a replacement order of
equal or greater value is placed simultaneously.

5.3 Consumables are non-returnable once the packaging seal is broken.

## 6. Delivery

6.1 Lead times are quoted ex-works Hong Kong.

6.2 Freight consolidation is available on a weekly sailing; consolidated
shipments add **7 to 10 days** to the quoted lead time.
