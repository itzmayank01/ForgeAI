# Supply Agreement — Nordhavn Marine AS

**Agreement reference:** SUP-NM-2026 · **Effective:** 1 February 2026 ·
**Term:** 24 months

## 1. Minimum order

1.1 The minimum order value is **EUR 750** excluding freight.

1.2 Orders below the minimum attract a small-order surcharge of **EUR 85**.

## 2. Volume discount tiers

2.1 Orders are discounted against the cumulative order value in a calendar
quarter:

| Quarterly cumulative value | Discount |
|---|---|
| EUR 0 – 9,999 | 0% |
| EUR 10,000 – 49,999 | **4%** |
| EUR 50,000 – 149,999 | **7.5%** |
| EUR 150,000 and above | **11%** |

2.2 Discounts are applied at invoice and are not retrospective across quarters.

## 3. Payment terms

3.1 Payment is due **30 days** from invoice date.

3.2 A settlement discount of **2%** applies to invoices paid within **10 days**.

3.3 Overdue amounts accrue interest at **0.8% per month**.

## 4. Warranty

4.1 All mechanical parts carry a **24 month** warranty from date of delivery.

4.2 Electrical components carry a **12 month** warranty.

4.3 Seals, gaskets and filter elements are consumables and carry a **6 month**
warranty against manufacturing defect only.

4.4 Warranty is void where parts have been installed outside manufacturer
torque specifications.

## 5. Returns

5.1 Unused stock items may be returned within **30 days** of delivery.

5.2 Returns attract a restocking fee of **15%** of the invoice value.

5.3 Special-order and non-catalogue items are non-returnable.

5.4 A return material authorisation number must be obtained before shipment.

## 6. Delivery

6.1 Quoted lead times run from receipt of a confirmed purchase order.

6.2 Nordhavn Marine does not guarantee delivery dates for items showing zero
stock at time of order.
